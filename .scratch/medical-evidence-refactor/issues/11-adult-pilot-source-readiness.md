## Question

For the confirmed single-condition, adult education and visit-preparation pilot, assess type 2 diabetes and hypertension using a small set of current primary sources. Verify current Indonesian national guidance, English/Indonesian patient education and visit-preparation resources, and one accessible primary study with interpretable reported numbers per candidate. Recommend a provisional topic based on source readiness, not unmeasured clinical safety or market demand. Check whether older Indonesian guidance has been replaced. Keep the investigation narrow; the user selects the topic and a full corpus review remains a later step.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-02 after review of [Adult pilot: source readiness](../../../docs/research/adult-pilot-source-readiness.md). Research snapshot: `research/adult-pilot-source-readiness`, report-only commit `c6a7a46`.

Both type 2 diabetes and hypertension have updated Indonesian adult PNPK guidance effective 17 April 2026; the actual documents explicitly replace older guidance. Public educational and study-summary sources exist for both. The checked diabetes education also directly supports record review and visit preparation, giving adult type 2 diabetes a provisional source-readiness recommendation. The user has not yet chosen the condition; this finding is neither a market-demand result nor clinical validation.

The report includes primary trial examples with interpretable reported numbers and flags important evidence constraints: study eligibility, null findings, updated analyses, an erratum, missing raw-data access and unverified commercial reuse rights. A government-hosted education page can still contain third-party content; a free-to-read manuscript is not necessarily licensed for reuse. MedlinePlus's encyclopedia visit-question content has explicit ingestion restrictions. Full provenance and sources live in the linked report. Source coverage, licenses, and bilingual content still need a complete audit after the topic decision.
