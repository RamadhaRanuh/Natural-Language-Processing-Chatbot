## Question

What does a representative current landscape of clinical evidence assistants, patient symptom tools, care services, and research assistants reveal about features specific to medical use? Compare intended audience, evidence traceability, study data, care escalation, personalization, drug information, privacy, and iPhone support using official sources. Separate documented features from marketing claims and unknowns. Which opportunities fit a patient-and-caregiver product whose central feature is inspectable claim-level support?

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-02 after review of [Medical chatbot landscape and product implications](../../../docs/research/medical-chatbot-landscape.md). Research snapshot: branch `research/medical-chatbot-landscape`, report-only commit `8a7e8f3`.

Thirteen current or historical offerings were compared across clinician reference, literature synthesis, patient assessment/care, wellbeing, and general-assistant health integrations. These are different intended uses. Documented medical-specific features include curated clinical sources, evidence-strength indicators, study snapshots, medication databases, structured intake, care navigation, human clinician review, visit preparation, and clinical workflow integration.

The original citation/verification idea overlaps vendor-described capabilities in OpenEvidence, ClinicalKey AI, and Consensus. The candidate product advantage is independently evaluated claim-to-passage support and numeric fidelity, presented through patient-readable bilingual evidence cards and doctor-visit preparation. This is a hypothesis to validate, not an established unique feature or an implementation decision.

Vendor capabilities and accuracy assertions are marked separately from external primary evaluations, whose versions and tasks differ. Availability in US listings does not imply availability in Indonesia. Woebot's retirement is documented; OpenEvidence privacy-policy access and some native-app/current-enrollment details remain explicitly unknown. Full sourcing and recommendations live in the linked report.
