## Question

Given the confirmed Indonesia first launch and English/Indonesian answers, define which local authoritative guidance and care-navigation resources apply, how local applicability is explained, and how translated medical claims retain their meaning and evidence links. Identify what must be researched locally before the pilot. Do not reopen the confirmed market/language choices without a requested change.

## Comments

- User selected English and Indonesian, then explicitly confirmed Indonesia first, during charting on 2026-10-02. The ticket now concerns local guidance and bilingual evidence handling, not the already answered market/language choice.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-03 using the user-authorized recommended decision.

Indonesia is the first market. Treat the current 2026 national adult diabetes PNPK as the local clinical reference; preserve jurisdiction and study-population limitations instead of importing overseas patient targets. Provide English/Indonesian interface and visit preparation now, retaining English research passages when medical translation is unreviewed. Local emergency guidance is a separate clinician-reviewed policy; classification/privacy review is a predeployment responsibility, not an asserted approval.
