## Question

Given the chosen adult type 2 diabetes education/visit-preparation role, evidence contract, and Indonesia/bilingual source policy, identify a minimal source corpus that covers the agreed question categories. Verify identities, current versions, corrections/retractions, accessible study passages and numbers, and permission for each planned operation including storage, retrieval, quotations, and translation. Separate links-only sources from ingestible ones. Identify coverage gaps and questions that must be withheld. Use the narrow pilot-source-readiness research as a starting point, not proof of corpus completeness or permitted reuse. Report coverage and constraints to inform architecture and evaluation decisions.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-03 using the user-authorized recommended decision.

The focused audit identified two adult diabetes RCT candidates with inspected CC BY4.0 full text and exact passage/data locators. The corpus preserves enrollment versus endpoint denominators, raw numeric precision/range formatting and attribution. ST2EP has an abstract/table discrepancy, visibly retained for review; Telescot has a correction and remains withheld without reviewed correction/source records. Both are unapproved for patient publication. Basic condition, guideline/drug-label and Indonesian medical claim coverage is not yet an approved ingestible corpus. The specification selects this honest, limited research-preview corpus and refusal behavior rather than claiming broad coverage. Full audit and candidate payload are linked in the implementation specification.
