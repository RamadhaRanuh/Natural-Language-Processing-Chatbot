# Patient medical information

This project serves people seeking understandable medical information and the people helping them with their care.

## Language

**Patient**:
A person seeking information about their own health or care.
_Avoid_: Medical professional, clinician

**Caregiver**:
A person helping someone else understand or manage their health and care.
_Avoid_: Clinician, proxy patient

**Patient-reported information**:
Health information supplied by a patient or caregiver, including symptom descriptions; it records what they reported rather than an independently established medical finding.
_Avoid_: Verified symptoms, confirmed findings

**Visit summary**:
A patient-reviewed account of reported symptoms, their timing, concerns, and questions prepared for a conversation with a clinician, without an inferred diagnosis or urgency score.
_Avoid_: Diagnostic report, triage result

**Safety guidance**:
Clinician-approved information about seeking appropriate help when a possible emergency is raised, distinct from routine symptom-based urgency scoring.
_Avoid_: Automated diagnosis, urgency score
